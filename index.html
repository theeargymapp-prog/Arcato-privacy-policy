Arcato — Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Arcato is a practice app for violinists and violists. Your music, your playing and your practice stay on your device. There is no account and no advertising, and nothing you play is uploaded. Anonymous usage statistics are sent: in Europe only after you say yes, elsewhere unless you turn them off.

The short version. Scores, photos, PDFs and your settings are stored only on your phone or tablet, and printed music is read on the device itself. The tools that listen turn sound into pitch as it arrives; what you choose to record stays on your device. Unless you turn it off in Settings (in Europe, only after you say yes), anonymous counts of which features are used are sent through Google Firebase. On Android, the free version sends a one-way hash of an app identifier and how many free uses it has had, so that reinstalling does not reset them. Delete the app and everything else goes with it.

Your scores stay on your device

Scores you scan, photograph, import or open from the bundled library are read and stored on the device itself. The reading is done by software inside the app; no page, photo, PDF or title is sent to the developer or to any other company. The same is true of your saved scores and your settings. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google's or Apple's), it may hold a copy of the app's data for you, under their terms.

The microphone

The tuner, Intonation Check, the drills and the vibrato tool listen through the microphone to tell you about your pitch, only while they are open and listening.

  • The sound is turned into pitch measurements on the device, as it arrives, and is not kept.
  • When you press Record (in the practice journal or the slow-downer), the recording is made and kept on your device: in the journal until you delete it, in the slow-downer only while the tool is open. Recordings leave the device only if you share them yourself.
  • No sound is ever sent to the developer or to any other company.

Android and iOS ask for the microphone permission the first time you use one of these tools. If you say no, every other part of the app still works.

No account, no advertising

There is no sign-in and no profile. Arcato shows no advertising, uses no advertising identifier, and nothing is sold.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To learn which features are used and how scans go, Arcato can send anonymous statistics through Google Firebase Analytics.

  • In Europe they are sent only after you say yes to a question in the app. Elsewhere they are on unless you turn them off. Settings → Share usage statistics changes it at any time; turning it off also deletes the identifier below from the app.
  • What is sent: which screens and tools are opened, choices from fixed lists (a drill's kind, an export's format), rounded counts and lengths, how a scan ended and measures of the page's image (its size, light and layout), whether Premium was bought, the app's version, the device's model, system, memory, language and region, and a random identifier made for this installation of the app.
  • What is never sent: sound or recordings, your pitch or how well you play, scores, titles, photos, files, anything you type, your name, your contacts or an advertising identifier.
  • Google processes this data for the developer (Google's privacy policy, Firebase and privacy), possibly outside your country. The IP address is used by Google to derive the rough location and is not stored. Events are kept for at most 14 months.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; the app is told only whether you own Premium, and remembers that on the device. The developer never sees your name, your card or your address.

Reading printed text (Android)

On Android, the title, the words and the part names printed on a page are recognised with Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The images and the recognised text stay on the device. Google's component may send Google technical information about itself, such as the device model and the version in use; it does not send your photos or the text it read.

Counting free uses

So that uninstalling and reinstalling the app, or clearing its data, does not reset the free scans, plays and intonation checks:

  • Android: the free app sends the developer's server (clefsnap.com, hosted by Namecheap) a one-way hash of your phone's Android ID (an identifier Android gives each app on each phone) together with the app's name and how many of each free use it has had. The server hashes it again with a secret key before storing it, keeps only those counts, and deletes them after 400 days without use. It is used only to count free uses (fraud prevention), never for advertising or analytics, and is not shared. Your IP address is not stored. Premium users send nothing. If the server cannot be reached, the app goes by its own counts.
  • iPhone and iPad: the same counts are kept in your device's Keychain, which iOS keeps after the app is deleted. They never leave your device.

Permissions, and what each is for

  • Microphone — for the tools that listen, and for recordings you make.
  • Camera — to photograph a page of music, when you choose to.
  • Photos and files you pick — only the ones you select; the app does not browse your library.
  • Notifications (Android) — to show the progress of a page being read, and that music, the drone or the metronome is playing, so you can leave the app while it works.
  • Running in the background (Android) — to finish reading a page, or to keep a score, the drone or the metronome sounding, when you leave the app or the screen is off.

Children

Arcato is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone; only the free-use count (Android) and the usage statistics described above leave the device.

Your rights and deleting your data

Everything except the Android free-use count and the usage statistics lives on your device; deleting the app deletes it. You can turn the statistics off in Settings, and withdraw a yes at any time. To have the free-use count deleted, write to the address below with your device model; as the server cannot link a count to you, it may ask you to send it from the app.
